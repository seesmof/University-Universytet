🧭 How to “know all about it”
---

To master the Vigenère cipher, you can explore it from multiple angles:

- Conceptual: Understand polyalphabetic substitution and why it was stronger than monoalphabetic ciphers.
- Practical: Implement it in code (Python, C#, or even Svelte for a web demo).
- Analytical: Study how it was broken (Kasiski examination, Friedman test, frequency analysis).
- Historical: See its role in Renaissance cryptography and why it was considered unbreakable for 300 years.
